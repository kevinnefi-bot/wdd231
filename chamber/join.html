NP Membership Benefits

Designed for non-profit organizations looking to network within the community at no cost.

  • Directory listing
  • Access to general networking events
  • Community support resources

Bronze Membership Benefits

Great for small businesses looking for foundational growth.

  • Directory listing with website link
  • 10% discount on event tickets
  • Access to chamber training workshops

Silver Membership Benefits

Designed for growing companies seeking increased publicity.

  • All Bronze benefits included
  • Rotating homepage spotlight listing
  • 20% discount on event tickets
  • 1 Free admission ticket to the Annual Gala

Gold Membership Benefits

Our premium package for industry leaders.

  • All Silver benefits included
  • Permanent homepage spotlight placement
  • Free banner advertising on directory
  • VIP seating at all chamber events
